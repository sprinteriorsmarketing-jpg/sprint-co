5 Tips/Tricks Restaurant Should Follow To Attract Customers

The restaurant industry faces fierce competition, with 70% of new restaurants closing within three to five years. However, establishments surviving past five years typically remain open for 10+ years — customer attraction and retention are the deciding factors.

The restaurant industry faces fierce competition, with 70% of new restaurants closing within three to five years. However, establishments surviving past five years typically remain open for 10+ years, indicating that customer attraction and retention are critical success factors.

Success requires more than quality food — strategic interior design and proactive customer engagement strategies are essential competitive advantages.

1. Do Your Promotions Traditionally and Digitally

Leverage both word-of-mouth and digital channels. Since customers are tech-savvy, utilise search engines, social media, and review sites to build consistent online visibility — one without the other leaves reach on the table.

2. Host Unique Events

Move beyond standard wine tastings and live music. Consider hosting workshops with interior designers, or collaborating with local business partners to showcase complementary products your regulars might not otherwise discover.

3. Free Wi-Fi

62% of diners spend a longer time at restaurants with free Wi-Fi, and 50% of them spend more money than at establishments without this service. Use this to manage slow periods through happy hours and loyalty programmes.

4. Create Niche Places

Design special zones — kids' play areas or smoking sections — that make customers feel privileged and encourage longer visits.

5. Invite Local Institutions

Host schools, colleges, and corporate groups with customised menus and entertainment to build community loyalty that pays off well beyond the event itself.

Be Creative in Your Outlook

Restaurant success depends on multifaceted strategies beyond food quality. Professional interior design creates memorable experiences that encourage repeat visits — pair it with the right engagement strategy, and both reinforce each other.
