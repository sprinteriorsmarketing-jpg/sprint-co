Talk to SprintCo About Your Project

Whether you are opening your first café, rolling out a QSR chain or refreshing an existing restaurant, the quickest way to start is to share a few details about your project. Our team will get back to you to understand your brief and suggest the next step.

What to share with us

  • Format: restaurant, café, bar, QSR, kiosk or cloud kitchen.
  • Location and site: the city, whether the site is in a mall, airport or on the high street, and whether it is a bare shell or an existing outlet.
  • Area: the approximate carpet area in sq ft.
  • Timeline: when you would like to open.
  • Budget: a rough range helps; you can work one out with our restaurant interior cost calculator.

What happens next

We review your details and arrange a call to understand the brief. If the site is confirmed, the next steps are usually a site survey, a concept and layout, and then a clear scope and bill of quantities before any work starts. You can appoint us for design only, or for design and build through to handover.
