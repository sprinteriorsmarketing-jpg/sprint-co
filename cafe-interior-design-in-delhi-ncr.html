Café Interior Designers in Delhi NCR

SprintCo works with café owners across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, and Faridabad to turn a raw space into a fully realized brand experience all the way from concept ideation and 3D renders to final layout, ready to hand off for construction.

Why Café Owners in Delhi NCR Choose SprintCo

Local market understanding
Delhi NCR’s café scene spans everything from Hauz Khas Village’s indie coffee spots to Gurugram’s mall-format QSR cafés. We design around the specific footfall, climate, and compliance realities of each micro-market and not a one-size-fits-all template.

Design + build, in one place
Working with SprintCo (concept, branding, 3D designs) and FlatPackCo (on-site fabrication and execution) means your café can go from first sketch to opening day without managing separate designer and contractor teams.

Built for how NCR cafés actually operate
Whether it’s a compact grab-and-go counter in a Noida sector market or a full-service café in a Gurugram high street, we design for real service flow including queue management, seating turnover, and kitchen access not just aesthetics.

Areas We Serve Across Delhi NCR

We provide cafe interior design service in the following areas around:

  • Delhi: Hauz Khas, Greater Kailash, Saket.
  • Gurugram: Cyber Hub, Sector 29, DLF areas
  • Noida (Sector 18, Noida Extension)
  • Faridabad and surrounding NCR townships

Our Café Design Process

1. Discovery: Understanding your brand story, target audience, and site constraints

2. Concept Design: Mood boards and layout direction aligned to your vision

3. 3D renders: A realistic visual of the finished café before construction starts

4. Detailed Drawings: Layout, electrical, and MEP-ready plans for execution

5. Build Handoff: Seamless transition to FlatPackCo for on-site fabrication and install

FAQ's
How much does café interior design cost in Delhi NCR?

Costs vary by café size, location, and finish level, generally it’s within the range of 400-800/sq. ft. SprintCo provides a detailed estimate after understanding your specific brief and site.

How long does café interior design take in Delhi NCR?

Design typically takes a few weeks, with full design-to-build achievable in as little as 45 days depending on site readiness.

Do you design cafés for malls as well as high-street locations?

Yes, we’ve designed for both formats, each with different compliance and layout considerations.

Can I get a 3D render before committing to construction?

Yes, every project includes a 3D render stage so you can visualize and approve the design before any physical work begins.

Who Designed Dolce Café?

Dolce Café in New Delhi was designed by SprintCo, the F&B interior design firm led by founder Sona Mantri.
