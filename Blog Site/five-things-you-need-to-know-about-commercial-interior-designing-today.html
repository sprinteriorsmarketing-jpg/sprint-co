Five Things You Need To Know About Commercial Interior Designing Today

No two commercial kitchens are alike. Whether you're planning a new build or a refurbishment, here are the fundamental design steps every owner should understand before breaking ground.

No two commercial kitchens are alike. Whether you're planning a new build or a refurbishment for your restaurant, understanding the fundamental design steps matters more than most owners expect going in.

Menu and Equipment

Materials in commercial kitchens should be smooth, continuous, suitable for contact with food, and easily washable and disinfectable. Equipment placement must prioritise efficient workflow and hygiene standards from the very first layout sketch.

Optimized Layout

Layout considerations include available space, lighting, and ventilation. Measure your dimensions before design begins, and know where windows, doors, and electrical outlets are — they matter significantly for workflow planning. Proper ventilation systems for smoke and fume removal are essential for staff wellness, not just comfort.

Easy Maintenance

Maintenance should be prioritised over pure aesthetics. Strategies include segregating areas by function — dishwashing kept separate from electrical appliances — and selecting low-maintenance materials that hold up under daily commercial use.

Energy Efficiency

The benefits extend beyond environmental advantages. Strategies include energy-efficient lighting, regular equipment maintenance, proper kitchen layout, and staff training on efficient protocols — all of which reduce your running costs month over month.

Local Codes and Safety Regulations

Safety compliance is critical. Designers must incorporate rules regarding storage, preparation, disposal, and fire safety to prevent hazards — these aren't suggestions, they're what determines whether you're allowed to operate at all.

Conclusion

Getting menu-driven equipment planning, layout, maintenance, energy efficiency, and safety compliance right from the start saves you from expensive rework and compliance headaches down the line.
