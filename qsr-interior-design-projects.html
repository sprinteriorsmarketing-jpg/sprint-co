QSR Interior Design Projects

Quick-service restaurants are judged on speed and consistency. The queue, the order point, the kitchen line and the pickup counter have to work at peak hour, and the design has to repeat across outlets without losing the brand. The QSR projects above show how SprintCo designs for that.

  • National brands: Pizza Hut in Mumbai, Domino’s Pizza across multiple locations and Haldiram in Bengaluru. For Pizza Hut, the fit-out was completed in 30 to 45 days with predictable costs.
  • Rollouts: The Croffle Guys, with 8 outlets live in Mumbai, including Charni Road, Matunga and Lokhandwala, each built in about 30 days against roughly 60 days for traditional construction. Also Sandoitchi Mumbai and Sandoitchi Ahmedabad.
  • Regional brands: Fly Chicken in Hyderabad, Dosa Coffee in New Delhi, Kanha Delight Express in Jaipur and Sandwizzaa in Kandivali.
  • Drive-thru: MB Chicken in Doraha, Punjab.

How we design a QSR

We map the order flow first: queue, order and payment, kitchen line, pickup and delivery handover, so the busiest hour runs smoothly. The kitchen is planned around the menu, and branding is built into the counter, menu boards and facade. For brands that plan to grow, we document the design as a repeatable kit of finishes and details, so new outlets open quickly and look the same.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can a QSR outlet be built?

With FlatPackCo’s off-site fabrication, The Croffle Guys outlets were built in about 30 days each, against roughly 60 days for traditional construction.

Can you roll out a QSR design across many outlets?

Yes. We create standards and a repeatable kit of parts so each new site is adapted, not redesigned. The Croffle Guys and Sandoitchi are examples.

How much does QSR interior design cost?

Do you design drive-thru outlets?

Yes. MB Chicken in Doraha, Punjab is a QSR and drive-thru project.
