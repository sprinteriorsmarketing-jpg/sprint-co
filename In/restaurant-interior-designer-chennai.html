Our Chennai Projects in Detail

Chennai’s dining culture is built around family meals, celebrations and loyalty to trusted names, and our Chennai project was designed for exactly that.

Zaitoon Signature, Nungambakkam · Restaurant and banquet hall

A ground-floor restaurant and a separate banquet hall run as two experiences within one address. The dining room pairs deep olive-green leather booths and cane-backed chairs with warm timber and brass-toned lattice screens, giving a layered, all-day warmth that suits a family lunch as well as a business dinner. One floor up, the banquet hall opens into a full event space with soft gold pendant lighting, built for weddings, birthdays and large gatherings without losing the restaurant’s identity.

Planning the two spaces together meant separating guest arrivals, kitchen support and service routes so a full banquet does not disrupt the restaurant below.

What Shapes an F&B Project in Chennai

Families and large groups

Chennai restaurants see a lot of family and group dining. Seating needs to flex from tables for two to long tables for ten without rebuilding the room.

Events as a revenue stream

Banquet and private-dining spaces, as at Zaitoon Signature, add income from celebrations, but need their own entry, service and acoustic planning.

Heat and humidity

Air-conditioning load, ventilation and moisture-resistant finishes matter in Chennai’s climate, especially for kitchens and outdoor seating.

Neighbourhood loyalty

Outlets in Nungambakkam, Anna Nagar, Besant Nagar and along OMR serve regulars. Comfort and consistency keep them coming back.

Approvals and rules to plan for in Chennai

Chennai projects typically need a trade licence from the Greater Chennai Corporation, an FSSAI food licence, fire safety clearance where the size or occupancy requires it and signage permissions. Tamil Nadu’s rules on serving alcohol are stricter than in many states, so if a bar is part of the concept, confirm licensing before the layout is fixed. Banquet spaces may need additional fire and occupancy approvals.
