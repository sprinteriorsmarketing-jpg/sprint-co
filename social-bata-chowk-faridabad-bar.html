Social Bata Chowk

Type Bar
Location Faridabad
Client Social
01 / 12

A factory-inspired hangout where scaffolding takes centre stage.

Raw. Rhythmic. Remarkably Local.

At Bata Chowk in Faridabad, an area powered by factories and machinery, we designed SOCIAL to echo the city's industrial pulse.

We turned a two-level scaffolding setup into both a statement and seating, blending exterior and interior into one bold experience. Gauges, meters, and wires complete the vibe, an ode to Faridabad's hardworking spirit.

Design Strategy

Structure A two-level scaffolding setup that doubles as both seating and statement
Detailing Gauges, meters, and wires as an ode to Faridabad's industrial spirit
Flow Exterior and interior blended into one bold, continuous experience

Frequently Asked Questions

Who designed SOCIAL Bata Chowk, Faridabad?

SOCIAL Bata Chowk, Faridabad was designed by SprintCo, under the leadership of Sona Mantri, Founder & CEO of SprintCo. The design celebrates Faridabad’s industrial heritage through bold architecture, raw materials, and immersive hospitality interiors.

What is the design concept behind SOCIAL Bata Chowk?

The design draws inspiration from Faridabad’s industrial history, incorporating factory-inspired elements, exposed materials, scaffolding details, and locally inspired storytelling to create a unique dining and social destination.

How does the layout improve the customer experience?

The layout is designed to support comfortable seating, clear customer circulation, efficient service operations, and multiple social zones for dining, working, and events.

How does lighting enhance the SOCIAL Bata Chowk ambience?

Layered lighting highlights architectural features, creates intimate seating areas, enhances the industrial character, and contributes to the outlet’s energetic atmosphere.
