ABOUT SPRINTCO

A Hospitality Design Firm, Built for F&B

It all started with a simple question, why do some restaurants thrive while others struggle?

We've spent over two decades designing and building F&B spaces, and one thing became clear: passionate restaurateurs often get stuck in the chaos of execution. They dream of a space that attracts customers, tells a story, and runs smoothly, but the process? Slow, expensive, and frustrating.

So we built SprintCo, a one-stop solution to take the guesswork out of restaurant design and build.

Today SprintCo is a hospitality design firm that specialises in food and beverage: restaurants, cafés, bars, QSRs, kiosks and family entertainment spaces. We have designed more than 3,500 F&B spaces for 400+ brands, from Pizza Hut and BrewDog to Blue Tokai and Social, with design, branding, MEP and build under one roof.

Our Solutions

We don't just create beautiful spaces, we design for function, flow, and customer experience.

Design – More than just aesthetics, we create spaces that feel good, flow well, and keep customers coming back.

Build (FlatPack Solutions) – Faster, smarter construction with modular solutions that take 50% less time than a traditional build. Less hassle, more efficiency.

Branding – Your restaurant isn't just a place, it's an experience. We make sure your brand stands out and sticks.

MEP Planning – Smooth operations start behind the scenes. We take care of the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing so everything runs seamlessly.

"I envisioned a hybrid brand that didn't just specialise in designing unique F&B spaces but also built with speed and precision."

Sona Mantri founded SprintCo with a vision to transform F&B spaces while solving real challenges for restaurant owners, from rising costs to execution inefficiencies.

Her first real exposure to design started with repairing a KFC outlet, which led her into restaurant design. Soon, brands like Pizza Hut and other multinational giants followed, drawn to her ability to turn challenges into seamless, functional spaces.

"I learned everything on the job—through mistakes, through client recommendations."

Over time, she built SprintCo into a powerhouse—growing from a one-woman army to leading a 100+ person team that designs and delivers F&B spaces across India.

With 30 years of experience, she has shaped some of the most recognised restaurant brands and, along the way, earned accolades such as Best Interior Designer in the F&B Industry and Outstanding Contribution in the F&B Industry (Female)

For Sona, great design isn't just about world-class aesthetics—it's about creating spaces that work and elevate experiences.
