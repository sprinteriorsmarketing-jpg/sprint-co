01  /  Situation & Challenge

The Croffle Guys & The Challenge

Four founders didn’t just open a café, they invented a category.

Amay Thakkar, Veer Pinto, Rahul Vohra and Annanya Agarwal built The Croffle Guys around a single hybrid product: the croffle, a croissant folded into waffle form. It wasn’t positioned as another dessert café , it was built to be the anchor of a new QSR category, aimed squarely at young, urban Indians who treat a snack run like a social event.

The first outlet had no professional designer behind it at all. It was self-built with the help of friends and family , a scrappy proof of concept that worked because the product and the founders’ instincts were right. That outlet proved the idea. It didn’t prove the brand could scale.

As demand grew and the founders set their sights on new Mumbai neighbourhoods, they came to SprintCo with what looked like a straightforward construction brief: help us build outlet two and three. The real problem was sitting one layer beneath that ask.

“We were supposed to work on just their build. But we realised that what they actually required was also to create a completely new design.”

Sona Mantri , Founder, SprintCo

Croffle Guys didn’t have a construction problem. They had a scaling problem. Every new outlet risked one of two failures: look identical and feel dated to the neighbourhood, or get reinvented from scratch and dilute the very brand identity that made outlet one work. The target guest , defined sharply in the brand’s own audience personas , would notice either mistake immediately.

02  /  Approach

The Strategy & Design Concept

Before drawing a single layout, SprintCo reframed the brief: this wasn’t a restaurant to design. It was a brand to systemise.

The team studied the existing brand equity closely , the personality Croffle Guys already had, the audience it was built for, the energy that made outlet one feel different. Rather than replacing that instinct, SprintCo doubled down on it and gave it a design language disciplined enough to travel.

At the centre of that language sits Sona Mantri’s own scaling framework, applied here for this brand: 70% brand DNA, 30% localisation. Seventy percent of the design, the palette, the materiality, the spatial logic, never changes. The remaining thirty percent flexes to the neighbourhood it’s built in.

“What works in Mumbai may not be the same as what works in Pune. You define what the brand is all about, and then you add the 30% which is localisation as per the location.”

Sona Mantri , Founder, SprintCo

Colour became the discipline that held it all together. Orange, already core to the Croffle Guys identity, was made the single loudest element in every space. Everything else, from the counter to the facade to the washrooms, was deliberately neutralised so nothing competed with it.

“We took that orange colour and had fun with it. Everything else was there to neutralise and support that hero we were trying to create.”

Sona Mantri , Founder, SprintCo

The brief also carried a clear repositioning: move away from the conventional café and build a genuine hangout. Seating was zoned for specific social moments , a window seat for friends who lose track of time, a corner for the regular who claims it and never gives it back, a counter for the after-work crowd rolling in like they already own the place. Signage was built to read like a treasure map, camera-ready by design, not by accident.

03  /  Delivery

Execution & Build

A design system is only as good as its ability to get built , fast, and without dilution.

This is where SprintCo paired design with FlatPackCo, our execution partner, to close the gap between a finished drawing and an open door. Rather than handing off the concept and hoping local contractors interpreted it correctly outlet after outlet, the same team that designed the system also owned building it.

The method was prototype-first: build and stress-test the system at one location, finalise every material and service detail with the client, confirm it holds up operationally, and only then replicate it , adapting the 30% that needed to flex for each address.

“Scale doesn’t happen when you’re designing the restaurant. Scale happens when you’re designing the system which is adaptable and replicated easily. Consistency beats complexity every single time.”

Sona Mantri , Founder, SprintCo

The payoff was speed. With nothing left to reinvent from scratch, each new Croffle Guys outlet moved from site to service in 30 to 45 days , turning the design system from an aesthetic upgrade into a genuine growth lever for the brand.

“What helped the most was the fact that we could execute these outlets at scale , that’s the best thing about having SprintCo and Flatpack on board.”

Rahul Vohra , Co-Founder, The Croffle Guys

The proof sits in three genuinely different Mumbai micro-markets, each carrying identical brand DNA with its own dial of local adaptation:

Charni Road

The system’s first live proof point , brand DNA established, prototype validated.

Matunga

Same design language, adapted to a different footfall and neighbourhood rhythm.

Lokhandwala

Third outlet, delivered on the same 30–45 day build cycle, consistency at pace.

05  /  Impact

Results & Impact

The real result here isn’t a nicer-looking café. It’s a brand that now owns a repeatable system to grow on.

  • A design system now exists where none did before, replacing one-off, ad hoc outlet builds with a documented, repeatable brand kit.
  • Three live outlets prove it holds up across genuinely different Mumbai neighbourhoods, Charni Road, Matunga and Lokhandwala, without diluting the brand.
  • Build timelines compressed to 30–45 days per outlet, removing the single biggest bottleneck to opening a new location.
  • No dependency on hiring a fresh local agency for every new neighbourhood or city, the design system itself now travels.
  • A brand built by four founders with no designer now has design as one of its core scaling assets, validated by the people who lived the transition.

“Thanks to Sona’s expertise and Kavita’s design capabilities, we can hopefully take Croffle nationwide, and have these designs, and the way these designs are consistent with our brand, flow on a nationwide level.”

Rahul Vohra , Co-Founder, The Croffle Guys

The Croffle Guys is no longer scaling a restaurant. It is scaling a system, and systems, unlike single restaurants, are built to travel.
