Founder & CEO, SprintCo

30 Years. One-Woman Army to 120+ Person Team.

Sona Mantri founded SprintCo to solve real problems for restaurant owners — rising costs and execution delays — with a hybrid model built for both design and speed.

Her first project was repairing a KFC outlet. That led her into F&B design, and soon brands like Pizza Hut and other multinational giants followed. Today she leads the team behind 3,500+ restaurants, cafes and bars across India and abroad.

Recognised with Best Interior Designer in the F&B Industry and Outstanding Contribution in the F&B Industry (Female).
