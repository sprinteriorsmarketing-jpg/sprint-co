How SprintCo Works Across Cities

SprintCo is based in Andheri East, Mumbai, and designs restaurants, cafés, bars, QSRs and kiosks across India and internationally. The city pages above cover the places where we have real projects, with the outlets we have designed there and what it takes to open an F&B space in that city.

Our largest bodies of work are in Mumbai, Bengaluru and Delhi NCR, with projects in cities including Hyderabad, Chennai, Goa, Ahmedabad, Surat, Jaipur, Indore, Lucknow, Raipur, Agra and Chandigarh. Outside India, Aminia in Dubai is one of our international restaurant projects.

Working in a city without an office

Design, branding and drawings are developed by our central team, and site visits are planned at the key stages: survey, setting out, mid-build and snagging. With FlatPackCo, counters, joinery and wall systems are fabricated off-site and shipped ready to install, which keeps quality consistent wherever the outlet is and reduces time on site.

What changes from city to city

  • Landlord rules: malls, airports and high streets each have their own fit-out rules, working hours and approvals.
  • Costs: labour-heavy work is often lower in tier 2 and tier 3 cities, while materials and equipment cost much the same. The cost calculator adjusts for this.
  • Local tastes: materials, colours and seating styles are adapted to the audience; see using local culture in restaurant design.
