Editorial Policy

Last reviewed: 30 October 2026
Publisher: SprintCo (B2B Designers Pvt. Ltd.)

Who writes our content

Articles and guides on sprint-co.com are written by the SprintCo Editorial Team: designers, project managers and marketing staff at SprintCo who work on restaurant, café, bar and QSR projects every day. Posts written by our founder, Sona Mantri, are credited to her by name.

Where our facts and figures come from

  • Our own projects: project details, photos, timelines and outcomes come from work SprintCo has designed and delivered.
  • Our published benchmarks: cost ranges and timelines reflect SprintCo's own project experience, and are presented as indicative, not as quotes.
  • Company facts: figures about SprintCo (spaces designed, brands, team, area delivered) match our fact sheet.
  • Third-party data: where we cite market research or regulations, we name the source.

How we review and update content

Guides are reviewed by a senior member of the design team before publishing. We review our main guides regularly and when costs, regulations or methods change. When an article is materially changed, we show an “Updated” date next to the original publish date.

Independence

We do not accept payment for mentions, reviews or links in our articles. Brands and suppliers named in our content are included because they are relevant, or because they are SprintCo clients, which we state where it applies.

Corrections

If you spot an error, email info@sprint-co.com with the page link and the correction. We review every report and fix confirmed errors promptly.
