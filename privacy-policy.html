Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27th May 2025
Company: B2B Designers Pvt. Ltd. (Operating under the brand name SprintCo)
Contact Email: info@sprint-co.com

At SprintCo, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website or communicate with us.

1. General Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Any information shared with us is handled securely, with complete transparency, and in accordance with the applicable Indian laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the SPDI (Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when voluntarily provided by you, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or brand name
  • Any project-related details shared via contact forms, emails, or direct communication

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, government IDs, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only for:

  • Responding to inquiries
  • Discussing projects and services
  • Providing requested information
  • Internal communication and documentation
  • Following up after client interaction (email, phone, or meetings)

We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

SprintCo's website may use basic cookies and analytics tools (like Google Analytics) to track non-personally identifiable data such as:

  • Site traffic and page visits
  • Device/browser type
  • Referral source

These cookies do not store any personal information. You can disable cookies via your browser settings if you prefer not to share this data.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information with outside parties for commercial gain. However, your data may be processed through secure third-party service providers (such as website hosting, CRM tools, or analytics platforms) strictly for operational purposes. All third-party platforms used are contractually obligated to protect your data.

6. Email Communication & Spam Policy

If you opt in to hear from us, we may send occasional emails related to:

  • Company updates
  • Service-related announcements

All such emails will come from info@sprint-co.com. You can unsubscribe from these communications at any time by replying to the email or contacting us directly.

We do not send spam or promotional emails without consent.

7. Information Security

We follow accepted security protocols to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. While no online platform is completely immune, we take commercially reasonable steps to safeguard your information.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your information from our systems

To make any such request, contact us at info@sprint-co.com.

9. Grievance Redressal

In compliance with Rule 5(9) of the SPDI Rules, we have appointed a Grievance Officer to address any privacy-related concerns.

Grievance Officer

Name: Veena Tandel

Email: veena.tandel@sprint-co.com

We aim to resolve all concerns within 30 business days of receipt.

10. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any significant changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally.
