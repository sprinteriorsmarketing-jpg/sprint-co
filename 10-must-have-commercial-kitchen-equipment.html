10 Must-Have Commercial Kitchen Equipment

A guide to set up a running restaurant kitchen. Selecting appropriate kitchen equipment proves essential whether you're establishing a new restaurant or renovating an existing one — the kitchen is the heart of your restaurant.

Selecting appropriate kitchen equipment proves essential whether you're establishing a new restaurant or renovating an existing one — the kitchen is the heart of your restaurant. Here are the ten equipment categories that need careful planning.

1. Cooking Equipment

Includes ovens, ranges, grills, fryers, and coffee brewers. Consider your menu requirements and proper power configuration before finalising your list — the wrong power setup can derail your entire kitchen build.

2. Refrigeration Equipment

Get the capacity and shape selection right for refrigerators, freezers, and ice machines to maintain food freshness and keep your cold chain unbroken.

3. Storage Equipment

Shelving, carts, and containers for organised ingredient management — good storage design directly reduces waste and speeds up prep.

4. Food Preparation Equipment

Prep tables, mixers, processors, and blenders to minimise preparation time and keep your kitchen moving during service.

5. Small-Ware Equipment

Knives, boards, bowls, and pans — the essential hand tools your kitchen staff use every single service.

6. Janitorial Equipment

Cleaning supplies including mops, bins, and dishwashers for food safety compliance. Budget for these properly — they're not an afterthought.

7. Fabricated Equipment

The non-mechanical elements — ventilation systems and ductwork — that most owners underestimate until they cause a problem.

8. Serving Equipment

Choose tableware based on your restaurant's seating capacity and cuisine type — it's part of the guest experience, not just a functional item.

9. Safety and Hygiene Equipment

UV cabinets, protective gear, fire extinguishers, and first aid kits. Safety of the staff is the most pivotal thing in any commercial kitchen.

10. Operational Equipment

POS systems and digital menus to enhance efficiency and reduce order errors during peak hours.

Conclusion

Getting kitchen equipment planning right the first time saves you from expensive rework later. We'd recommend professional consultation for these decisions rather than guessing.
