The brief

What a bar needs that a restaurant doesn’t.

A bar is designed around the counter and the evening. These are the decisions we settle first:

  • Bar counter and back bar: length, service stations, glass washing, under-counter refrigeration and display, sized for peak hour
  • Draught and brewing: keg room, python lines, tap towers and, for brewpubs, the brewing kit and its services
  • Zoning: standing room, high tables, lounge seating, booths and group areas, so the room fills naturally
  • Layered lighting: from early evening to late night, with dimmable scenes for the bar, tables and feature walls
  • Acoustics: for music and conversation, without sound bleeding to neighbours
  • Durable finishes: counter tops, flooring and upholstery that handle spills and heavy use
  • Screens and entertainment: sight lines for sport, live music or games, as at Amoeba and DDUCKPIN
  • Licensing layout: drawings that support the operator’s excise licence application
How we work

From site to opening day.

  1. Site and licence check
    Ceiling height, structure for brewing or heavy equipment, exhaust, and whether the site suits an excise licence.
  2. Concept and bar planning
    The bar counter, back bar and service flow first, then the room around them.
  3. Branding and atmosphere
    Graphics, signage, murals and lighting that give the bar its character.
  4. MEP and acoustics
    Power for cold rooms and screens, drainage for bar stations, ventilation and sound.
  5. FlatPack fabrication
    Bar counters, back bars, booths and facades made off-site while services work happens on site.
  6. Install, snag and open
    Fast installation, lighting scenes set, and a detailed snag list before launch.

Budget

What does a bar interior cost?

Bars usually cost more per sq ft than restaurants because of the bar counter, back bar, acoustics and lighting. As a planning benchmark for a bar on a bare shell in a metro (interiors only, before draught or brewing equipment, licences and GST):

Finish levelIndicative range
Budget / functional₹920–1,380 per sq ft
Mid-range₹1,725–2,875 per sq ft
Premium₹3,220–5,750+ per sq ft

Ranges are lower in tier 2 and tier 3 cities on labour-heavy work. Get a figure for your own area, format and city with the restaurant interior cost calculator.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does it take to build a bar?

It depends on size and scope. With FlatPackCo fabricating counters, joinery and facades off-site, SOCIAL E-City (5,500 sq ft) was built in 65 days and Poee & Co in Goa was assembled on site in 22 days.

How much does bar interior design cost in India?

As a metro planning benchmark, about ₹920–1,380 per sq ft for a budget bar, ₹1,725–2,875 mid-range and ₹3,220–5,750+ premium, interiors only. Choose “Bar” in the cost calculator for your area and city.

Do you design brewpubs?

Yes. BrewDog Mumbai and BrewDog Chandigarh are brewpubs, planned around the brewing and serving set-up.

Do you handle the bar licence?

The excise licence is applied for by the operator, but we prepare the layout and drawings the application needs and plan the bar to suit it.

Can you design sports bars and entertainment bars?

Yes. Amoeba Sports Bar in Bengaluru and DDUCKPIN, a bowling bistro and bar in Malad, combine games and entertainment with food and drink.

Do you design bars outside Mumbai?

Yes. Our bar projects are in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chandigarh, Faridabad and Goa, and we work pan-India.
