Portfolio · Cafe & Bar

Spaces built for connection

In the Café & Bar segment, we focus on creating dynamic spaces that blend social interaction with functionality. From cozy cafes to vibrant bars, our designs cater to both casual lounging and lively socializing. Each space is crafted to enhance customer engagement while reflecting the brand's unique identity, offering a welcoming atmosphere that invites patrons to relax, connect, and enjoy.

01 / 06
Location: Faridabad, Haryana

SOCIAL

In the heart of Faridabad, Sprinteriors transformed a 3,160 sq ft space in Pebble Downtown Mall into a unique SOCIAL outlet. Merging the city's industrial roots with SOCIAL's modern identity, the interiors feature Haryanvi colloquialisms to create a hyperlocal ambiance, scaffolding-inspired structures reflect Faridabad's urban development.

This outlet offers SOCIAL's signature community-driven experiences, co-working facilities, and a welcoming space for all to connect and create memorable moments.

01 / 07
Location: Mumbai

The Beer Cafe

For The Beer Cafe, we created an inviting social hub that merges modernity with comfort. Our design approach focused on enhancing the communal atmosphere while showcasing a diverse selection of local and international brews.

With a layout that encourages interaction and a warm, eclectic ambiance, we helped The Beer Cafe solidify its status as a destination for friends to gather and enjoy their favorite beers.

01 / 04
Location: Vapi, Gujarat

Coffee Culture

In every Coffee Culture outlet, we crafted a vibrant, welcoming space that resonates with coffee lovers, families, and youth alike.

With a focus on creating an engaging customer experience, the design merges contemporary aesthetics with the brand's signature offerings of coffee, burgers, and fresh dough pizzas.

01 / 03
Location: Chembur, Mumbai · Mall Outlets

Third Wave Coffee

At Third Wave Coffee, SprintCo captured the essence of the 'Third Wave movement' by designing spaces that offer a premium coffee experience from farm to cup.

The focus was on creating a welcoming environment that fosters community and conversation, with thoughtful design elements that reflect the brand's dedication to quality and sustainability. Each outlet is a testament to the art of coffee, blended with modern aesthetics.

01 / 06
Location: Powai, Mumbai

SanDoitchi

At SprintCo, we crafted a premium space for Sandoitchi in Powai, blending luxury with storytelling. Spatula motifs and sleek interiors reflect the artisanal nature of their sandwiches, creating a unique dining experience that feels both upscale and inviting.

01 / 04
Location: Agra

Karchi & Chow

We crafted a space that mirrors the rich culinary diversity of its menu. Our design brings together the bold spices of Pan Asian cuisine with the hearty flavors of North Indian dishes, creating a dining experience that feels both familiar and adventurous.

The layout encourages exploration, while the vibrant interiors ensure a memorable and engaging experience for every visitor.

01 / 04
Location: Across Mumbai · 9 Outlets

Sandwizzaa

Sandwizzaa is Mumbai's beloved sandwich brand, renowned for its commitment to quality, taste, and a unique blend of flavors that have made it a local favorite since 1986.

As the brand recognized the impact of our work, we were entrusted with the design of 9 outlets across the city. Each location was crafted to reflect Sandwizzaa's commitment to quality, providing a seamless and inviting customer experience that aligns with their brand ethos.

01 / 04
Location: Bangalore

Mojoco

Located on Bangalore's bustling Church Street, Mojoco is a 3-in-1 Café-Resto-Bar. SprintCo designed the "Mo" café space with a light-hearted, classy aesthetic, using neutral colors, green elements, and contemporary lighting to create an inviting and impactful entrance.
