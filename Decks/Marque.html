Our Work
Process

How We Work

From first idea to opening day

Every SprintCo project moves through the same nine-stage process. Here is exactly what that looked like for The Hood in Bengaluru, one of our Marque Projects, from the first mood board to the final photograph.

01

Ideation

Every project begins with research, not sketches. We study the brand's world, cultural references, era-appropriate artefacts, materials and mood, and translate it into a clear design direction before anything is drawn to scale.

01 / 10
02

3D Visualization

The direction is built out into full photorealistic 3D visualization, facade, interior, outdoor and basement, every angle rendered so the client can walk through and approve the complete spatial experience before construction starts.

01 / 15
03

GFC Drawing

Good-For-Construction drawings turn the approved design into precise, buildable documentation, floor plans, furniture layouts and seating counts our execution teams build directly from, with zero ambiguity on site.

01 / 02
04

Branding

A complete visual identity is built alongside the space itself, logo, colour system, signage and collateral, so the brand feels consistent everywhere a guest encounters it, from the entrance mark to the visiting card.

01 / 03
05

Site Execution

On-ground teams bring the drawings to life, coordinating civil work, MEP, carpentry and finishing trades, with our design team supervising on site to make sure every detail matches the approved design.

01 / 10
06

Decor Layering

The final layer of personality goes in last: art, artefacts, plants, lighting fixtures, upholstery and styling details that turn a finished shell into a space with history and character.

01 / 09
07

Pre-Launch Checklist

Before opening day, every element is walked through against a detailed checklist, snagging, equipment testing, staff walkthroughs and final styling, so nothing is missed when the doors open.

08

Launch Support

SprintCo stays engaged through opening week, on-site or on-call, to troubleshoot, fine-tune and support the team through the space's first days of live service.

Our involvement doesn't stop at handing over the keys. We plan and run the restaurant's launch event itself, guest lists, media invites, on-ground coordination and execution on the night, so the space gets a proper opening in front of the audience that actually matters to the brand.

Once the doors are open, we carry that momentum into marketing. SprintCo manages the restaurant's marketing after launch, social media, content and promotional campaigns, helping the brand build visibility and footfall well beyond the first week of service.

09

Final Photos

The finished space, captured, the result of ideation, design, drawings, branding and execution coming together exactly as envisioned.
