Frequently Asked Questions

Who designed the Domino’s Pizza outlet interior?

The Domino’s Pizza outlet interior in Goa was designed by SprintCo, an F&B design and build company specializing in QSRs, restaurants, cafés, and food courts. The design emphasizes operational efficiency, customer convenience, and brand consistency.

Why is interior design important for a quick-service restaurant like Domino’s Pizza?

Effective interior design improves operational efficiency, enhances customer comfort, strengthens brand identity, and creates an organized environment that supports faster service.

Who can design QSRs similar to Domino’s Pizza?

SprintCo designs quick-service restaurants, cafés, food courts, and hospitality spaces across India, creating interiors that balance operational efficiency, customer experience, and brand identity.

What materials are commonly used in the Domino’s Pizza interior?

The design incorporates durable laminates, easy-maintenance flooring, hospitality-grade furniture, decorative lighting, and robust finishes suitable for high-footfall restaurant environments.
