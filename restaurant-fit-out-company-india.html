What a fit-out includes

Everything between the bare site and opening day.

A restaurant fit-out turns an empty or existing space into a working F&B outlet. Depending on your site and brief, our fit-out scope can cover:

  • Civil and structural works: partitions, flooring, ceilings, waterproofing and kitchen floors
  • MEP services: electrical, lighting, plumbing, drainage and grease traps
  • Kitchen exhaust and HVAC: hoods, ducting, fresh air and air conditioning
  • Fire safety: sprinklers, detection, egress and fire-rated finishes
  • Joinery and counters: bar and service counters, display, storage and back bar
  • Finishes and furniture: wall finishes, upholstery, loose and fixed seating
  • Kitchen equipment coordination: layout, utilities and installation sequencing
  • Signage and branding: facade, menu boards and in-store brand elements
Fit-out types

Turnkey, shell-and-core or refurbishment.

Every site starts from a different point. We scope the fit-out around what the landlord hands over and what your brand needs.

Shell-and-core fit-out

A bare site with basic structure and services at the boundary. We plan and coordinate everything inside: services, kitchen, interiors and signage.

Turnkey fit-out

One team from approved design to handover, so you deal with a single point of contact, one programme and one budget instead of separate contractors.

Refurbishment and re-fit

Refreshing or rebranding an existing outlet, often in phases or overnight to keep trading. See what to plan before you refurbish.

How a fit-out runs

A clear fit-out programme, start to handover.

  1. Site survey and feasibility
    Electrical load, exhaust route, drainage, ceiling height and landlord rules checked before anything is drawn.
  2. Design and working drawings
    Approved layout, MEP and kitchen drawings that the site team can build from.
  3. BOQ and costing
    A line-by-line bill of quantities, so the budget is fixed before work starts.
  4. Approvals coordination
    Mall, society or landlord work permits, and the drawings your licence and fire applications need.
  5. Site works and FlatPack fabrication
    Civil and services on site while joinery and modules are made off-site in parallel.
  6. Finishes, snagging and handover
    Installation, lighting and signage, then a detailed snag list and handover ready for launch.
