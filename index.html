Restaurant interior design & build

One team from first sketch to opening day

SprintCo is a Mumbai-based design-and-build studio for restaurants, cafés, bars, QSRs and kiosks. We have delivered more than 3,500 F&B spaces and over 8 million sq ft for 350+ brands across India, from single-outlet founders to national chains such as Pizza Hut, BrewDog, Social and Blue Tokai.

Most restaurant projects go wrong in the gap between the designer and the contractor: a drawing that looks right but can't be built on budget, a kitchen that doesn't match the menu, or services that clash with the ceiling. We close that gap by handling the whole job ourselves. The same team that writes your brief also draws the layout, plans the kitchen and services, builds the site and hands over the keys, so there is one point of contact, one budget and one timeline.

The studio was founded by Sona Mantri, who has spent three decades shaping restaurant brands in India. That experience shows up in the details owners care about: seat counts that make the numbers work, kitchens that keep up on a full Saturday night, and interiors guests remember and photograph.

  • Concept design: layout, seating plan, materials, lighting and 3D visuals
  • Brand identity: logo, signage, menus and packaging that match the space
  • Kitchen planning: workflow, equipment and bar layouts sized to your menu
  • MEP design: electrical, plumbing, HVAC, exhaust and fire services
  • Build and fit-out: civil, carpentry, finishes and site management
  • FlatPack build: modular, factory-made interiors up to 50% faster on site
  • Rollout and expansion: repeatable formats for multi-city growth
  • Renovation: refreshing tired outlets with minimal downtime

How it works

Our design-and-build process

A clear sequence with sign-offs at each stage, so you always know what is being built, what it costs and when it will be ready.

  1. Brief & site reviewWe visit the site, study your menu, target guests and budget, and check the space against the services and approvals it will need.
  2. Concept & layoutSpace planning, seating and kitchen layout, then a design concept with materials, lighting and 3D views for your approval.
  3. Detailed design & costingWorking drawings, MEP design and a line-by-line bill of quantities, so the budget is fixed before work starts.
  4. BuildOur site team handles civil work, carpentry, services and finishes, using FlatPack modules where they save time.
  5. Handover & rolloutSnagging, equipment checks and handover ready for launch, plus a repeatable kit of parts if you plan more outlets.

FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask us

What does a design-and-build company do?

It handles both the design and the construction of your outlet under one contract. Instead of hiring a designer, then finding contractors to build the drawings, you get one team responsible for the concept, kitchen and services design, costing, site work and handover. That means fewer gaps between drawings and reality, and one party accountable for budget and timeline.

How long does it take to design and build a restaurant?

It depends on size, site condition and approvals. Small cafés and kiosks move fastest; full-service restaurants and bars with heavy kitchen and services work take longer. Our FlatPack modular build can cut on-site time by up to 50%. We give you a project schedule with the costing, before work starts.

How much does restaurant interior design cost per sq ft?

Our published benchmarks are roughly ₹800–₹1,200 per sq ft for budget setups, ₹1,500–₹2,500 for mid-range interiors and ₹2,800–₹5,000+ for premium concepts. Kitchen equipment, HVAC and licences are usually extra. See our cost guide for details.

Do you work outside Mumbai?

Yes. We deliver projects across India, including Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Goa and Ahmedabad, and have completed work in Dubai. See all the cities we work in.

Can you help us roll out multiple outlets?

Yes. We design a repeatable format, with standard layouts, specifications and modular elements, so new outlets open faster and look consistent. Many of the 350+ brands we work with are multi-outlet chains.

Do you design the kitchen and bar as well?

Yes. Kitchen and bar planning is part of every project: workflow, equipment selection and services are designed together with the dining area, so the back of house matches the menu and volume you expect.
