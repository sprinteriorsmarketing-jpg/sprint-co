The brief

What makes a kiosk work.

A kiosk has to do everything a full outlet does in a fraction of the space. These are the decisions we settle first:

  • Service side: where guests queue, order, pay and collect, without blocking the corridor
  • Display: lit, glass-fronted display that sells the product from a distance, as at Cookie Man and Dunkel Braun
  • Equipment and storage: every machine, fridge and bin placed to a real work sequence, with storage built in
  • Power, water and drainage: confirmed with the landlord before the design is fixed
  • Branding from every side: signage that reads across a concourse or atrium, often 360 degrees
  • Landlord rules: height limits, fire rules, approved materials and the fit-out window
  • Optional seating: a small cluster or lounge that extends the kiosk, as at Haldiram Cafe
  • Fast installation: off-site fabrication with FlatPackCo, so the kiosk arrives ready to assemble
How we work

From site to opening day.

  1. Site and landlord brief
    Footprint, services points, height limits and the mall, airport or campus fit-out rules.
  2. Layout and equipment
    Service side, equipment, storage and display planned to a real work sequence.
  3. Shell and branding
    The kiosk shape, materials and signage that make it stand out.
  4. Drawings and approvals
    Drawings prepared for the landlord’s approval and services coordination.
  5. Off-site fabrication
    The kiosk built in the FlatPackCo factory while the site is prepared.
  6. Install and open
    Quick assembly in the approved working hours, snagging and handover.

Budget

What does a kiosk cost?

Kiosks cost more per sq ft than a restaurant, because the counter, services, lighting and signage are spread over a small area, but the total is much lower. As a planning benchmark in a metro (interiors and shell only, before equipment, landlord charges and GST):

Finish levelIndicative range
Budget / functional₹1,040–1,560 per sq ft
Mid-range₹1,950–3,250 per sq ft
Premium₹3,640–6,500+ per sq ft

Ranges are lower in tier 2 and tier 3 cities on labour-heavy work. Get a figure for your own area, format and city with the restaurant interior cost calculator.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a food kiosk cost in India?

As a metro planning benchmark, about ₹1,040–1,560 per sq ft at a budget finish, ₹1,950–3,250 mid-range and ₹3,640–6,500+ premium. A 150 sq ft mid-range kiosk works out at roughly ₹2.9–4.9 lakh. Choose “Kiosk” in the cost calculator.

Why does a kiosk cost more per sq ft than a restaurant?

Because the counter, services, lighting, signage and display are fixed costs spread over a very small area.

Can a kiosk be built off-site?

Yes. Cococart at Mumbai Airport T2 was built off-site and assembled on site, which keeps installation short in malls and airports with limited working hours.

Do you design kiosks for airports?

Yes. Tea Tapri at Lucknow Airport, Cococart at Mumbai Airport T2 and Streetfoods by Punjab Grill at Bengaluru Airport are airport kiosks. See our airport F&B page.

Can a kiosk include seating?

Yes. Cookie Man at BKC has a small seating cluster, and Haldiram Cafe in Indore opens into a full lounge.

Do you handle mall approvals?

We prepare the drawings and coordinate with the landlord’s fit-out team; the operator holds the lease and licences.
