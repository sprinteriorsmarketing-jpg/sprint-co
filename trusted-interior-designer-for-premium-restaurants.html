Sona Mantri – Trusted Interior Designer for Premium Restaurants & Bars in India

1.A Journey That Started with a Broken KFC Outlet

Sona didn’t enter design through the conventional route.

Her first tryst with the world of F&B interiors began not with a glamorous project—but with repairing a KFC outlet. That repair job revealed her instinct for functional layouts, problem-solving, and creating spaces that just work. It also opened doors.

Soon, brands like Pizza Hut and other multinational chains sought her out—not for aesthetics alone, but for the way she turned operational challenges into seamless customer experiences.

“I learned everything on the job—through mistakes, through client recommendations.”
 This humility, paired with relentless execution, became her competitive advantage.

The Birth of SprintCo — A Hybrid Brand Built for Speed, Scale & Precision

Sona’s vision was clear from the start:

“I envisioned a hybrid brand that didn’t just specialise in designing unique F&B spaces but also built with speed and precision.”

This vision led to the creation of SprintCo, her design-and-build firm built to solve the real, everyday struggles of restaurant owners—delays, rising costs, scalability issues, and execution inefficiencies.

What began as a one-woman army soon expanded into a 100+ member powerhouse, capable of handling multi-city rollouts, high-pressure deadlines, and diverse formats—from premium bars to QSRs and fine-dining restaurants.

FAQ's
What is the biggest mistake restaurant owners make when hiring an interior designer?

The biggest mistake is hiring a generalist residential designer for a commercial F&B space. Unlike general interior designers, F&B specialists understand operational workflows, service efficiency requirements, and hospitality industry durability standards — knowledge that directly prevents costly mistakes during and after construction

What types of restaurants has Sona Mantri designed?

Over three decades, Sona has shaped some of India’s most recognised restaurant brands. Her work spans premium bars, QSRs, fine dining restaurants, and multi-city rollouts across diverse formats — every space carrying her signature approach of aesthetics rooted in utility

How long does it typically take to design and build a restaurant interior?

Most projects are completed within 30 to 45 days. This is possible because SprintCo operates as an integrated design-build firm, meaning the design and construction teams work in parallel rather than in sequence. There is no handover gap, no miscommunication between separate vendors, and no delays waiting for drawings to be translated into execution. For restaurant owners working against a lease start date or a planned launch, this timeline is a significant operational advantage.

How does Interior Designer Firm approach space optimisation for restaurant interiors?

Space optimisation is one of the core principles behind every project. The goal is never just to make a space look good, but to ensure every square foot earns its place. That means designing for efficient service flow, maximising covers without compromising the guest experience, and building in storage and operational zones that kitchens and floor staff can actually work with. A well-optimised layout directly affects table turnover, staff efficiency, and ultimately, profitability.

Can Interior Designer Firm manage restaurant design projects across multiple cities or for franchise rollouts?

Yes. SprintCo has executed design and build projects across multiple cities and has experience working with brands that need consistent rollouts at scale. The hybrid design-build model is particularly well-suited to franchise and multi-location projects, where maintaining brand standards across different physical spaces and local vendor ecosystems is a real challenge. The same design language, the same execution rigour, and the same delivery timelines apply regardless of geography.
